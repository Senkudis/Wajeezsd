# Merchant tutorial screens

Produced by `node tools/shots-merchant.js [names]`. Every shot is 390 CSS px wide at dpr 2.5 (975 px).
Normal shots are 390x844 CSS. Demo data: store مطعم الساحة (owner أحمد محمد), Khartoum; tier `pro` everywhere.
Product photos, the store cover, logo and transfer receipt are flat SVG drawings from `tools/merchant-art.js`.

**Marks in the .json files:** coordinates are in screenshot CSS px. When a shot scrolled before capture, `scrollY` is
stored and has already been subtracted. On fullPage shots, sticky headers are switched to `position: relative` so they
stay at the top of the tall image; fixed bars (bottom nav, FAB) are hidden.
