Version 1.4.3 (build 41). Previous approved version: 1.4.2.

WHAT IS NEW
The one substantive change is background location for the driver side only
(UIBackgroundModes: location). The rest is bug fixes and interface work.

BACKGROUND LOCATION — WHO, WHEN, WHY

wajeez is a local delivery app with two kinds of users: customers who order, and
drivers ("captains") who deliver. Background location applies to drivers only.

Who: only a signed-in driver account. Customers are never tracked in the
background. The customer side asks for location in the foreground only, to sort
nearby stores and place the delivery pin, and the app stays fully usable if
location is denied.

When: the driver taps the shift switch ("ابدأ الوردية" — start shift; the header
then reads "متصل" / online). From that moment until they turn it off, position
is shared. Turning it off stops tracking immediately. Nothing is collected while
a driver is offline.

Why it must continue in the background: a driver rides a motorbike with the
phone in a pocket or on a mount, screen locked. Without background location the
position freezes the moment the screen turns off, and two things break:

1. The customer watching the live map sees a frozen marker and concludes the
   driver has stopped or abandoned the order. This was our most common support
   complaint before this version.
2. Dispatch offers each new order to the nearest available drivers. A stale
   position means orders go to drivers who are no longer nearby.

Control: the shift switch is on the driver's main screen and is the single
control — one tap starts, one tap stops. While offline the screen shows a banner
saying the shift is stopped and no orders will arrive, so the state is never
ambiguous.

Data handling: positions go to our own server only, are used for live tracking
and dispatch, and are not shared with or sold to third parties. The app
processes no payments; every order is cash on delivery.

HOW TO VERIFY

1. Menu > "دخول الكابتن" (driver login), sign in with the driver account below.
2. Tap "ابدأ الوردية" (start shift) and allow the location permission.
3. Background the app or lock the screen.
4. Sign in as the customer account, place an order, open the tracking screen:
   the driver marker keeps moving. Tapping the shift switch again stops it.

STILL TRUE FROM THE APPROVED 1.4.2

Nothing here changes the points raised on earlier submissions:

- Browsing without an account works: with no account a user can open the Shop
  tab, all categories, any store page with products, prices and ratings, and
  search. Registration is requested only for cart, ordering, history, favourites
  and chat.
- No highly regulated field. No healthcare and no pharmacies — medicines are not
  sold, listed or searchable in any form. No banking or financial services: the
  app processes no payments, every order is cash on delivery, no card or wallet.
  No gambling, no air travel.
- Goods come from independent local businesses (restaurants, groceries,
  bakeries, cafés, clothing, beauty) that register with us and remain
  responsible for their products and trade licences. We sell nothing and hold no
  inventory.
- The onboarding form collecting a bank account and an ID document is shown only
  to business owners applying to sell, never to customers: the account is where
  we transfer a merchant their own sales proceeds, the ID verifies that the
  person registering a store owns it. Both are visible only to administrators
  and are never returned by any public API.
