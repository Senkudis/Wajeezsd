Version 1.4.3 — what changed and why background location is used

NEW IN THIS VERSION: BACKGROUND LOCATION (UIBackgroundModes: location)

wajeez is a local delivery app with two kinds of users: customers who order,
and drivers ("captains") who deliver. Background location is used by the
driver side only.

Who is tracked
Only a signed-in driver account. Customers are never tracked in the background;
the customer app requests location only in the foreground, to sort nearby stores
and to place the delivery pin, and the app is fully usable with location denied.

When it runs
The driver opens the app and turns on the "Online" switch to start a shift. From
that moment until they switch it off, their position is shared. Turning the
switch off stops tracking immediately. No location is collected while a driver
is offline, and none is collected for customers.

Why it must continue in the background
A driver rides a motorbike with the phone in a pocket or on a mount with the
screen locked. Without background location the position freezes the moment the
screen turns off. Two things break:
1. The customer watching the live map sees a frozen marker and concludes the
   driver has stopped or abandoned the order. This was our most common support
   complaint before this version.
2. Order dispatch sends each new order to the nearest available drivers. A stale
   position means orders are offered to drivers who are no longer nearby.

What the driver sees
While tracking is active, Android shows a persistent notification ("You are
online"), so the driver always knows location sharing is on and can stop it with
one switch inside the app.

How to verify
Sign in with the driver account below (in-app menu > "دخول الكابتن"), turn on
the "Online" switch, and accept the location permission. The position updates
are visible in the customer's tracking screen for an active order.

Data handling
Positions are sent to our own server only, used for live order tracking and
dispatch, and are not shared with or sold to third parties. The app processes no
payments; every order is cash on delivery.
