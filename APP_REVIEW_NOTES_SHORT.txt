GUIDELINE 5.1.1(v) — REGISTRATION BEFORE BROWSING
You are right, and our previous reply was incomplete — we apologise. We had removed one registration gate but a second one remained on the main Shop tab, which is the screen your reviewer opened: the category grid was replaced by a card reading "you must sign in to view stores". That gate is removed.

GUIDELINE 2.1 — INFORMATION NEEDED
Which companies or institutions provide the services?
wajeez is a local delivery platform. We sell nothing ourselves and hold no inventory. Goods are supplied by independent local businesses — restaurants, groceries, supermarkets, bakeries, cafés — which register, are reviewed by us, and remain responsible for their products and their own trade licences. We provide the ordering interface and delivery by contracted drivers.

The app does not operate in any highly regulated field: no healthcare and no pharmacies (medicines are not sold, listed or searchable in any form; an earlier build listed a pharmacy category, removed in this build); no banking or financial services (the app processes no payments — every order is cash on delivery, no card or wallet); no gambling and no air travel.

About the bank details and ID document in your screenshot: that is our merchant onboarding form. It is collected only from business owners applying to sell, never from customers. The bank account is where we transfer a merchant their own sales proceeds; the ID verifies that the person registering a store is its owner. These fields are visible only to our administrators and are never returned by any public API.

What is the relationship between Mohamed Hamza and the providers of these services?
There is no ownership relationship. He holds no interest in any merchant listed in the app; each is an independent seller under a standard platform-merchant agreement. Mohamed Hamza is the app's developer and the holder of this developer account, acting for wajeez, registered in Sudan as a business under the name "تطبيق وجيز للتوصيل" ("Wajeez Delivery Application"). The registration certificate can be provided on request.
