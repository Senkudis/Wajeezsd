2. PURPOSE AND TARGET AUDIENCE
Wajeez is a local delivery service in Sudan (Khartoum and Port Sudan). Today
deliveries are arranged by phone with individual couriers: prices are haggled
verbally, the customer cannot see where the parcel is, and no record survives
if something goes wrong. Wajeez replaces that. A customer marks pickup
and drop-off on a map and says what they need; nearby couriers ("captains")
accept the listed price or propose another; the customer chooses, follows the
captain live on the map, and pays cash on delivery. Customers can also browse
local stores, order products, and chat with the captain or store.
Audience: adults in Sudan needing parcels, groceries or restaurant orders
delivered, and small merchants selling beyond walk-in customers. Rated 4+, no
age-restricted content. A public consumer app, not an employee-only one.

CONTENT REPORTING AND BLOCKING (Guideline 1.2). The app shows user-written
store reviews, so both mechanisms exist:
- Report a review: open a store, tap "Customer Reviews", tap the report
  button under a review, pick a reason, submit.
- Report or block a user: open a chat, tap the header menu, then report abuse
  or block user. A blocked user can no longer exchange messages with the
  person who blocked them.
- Reports reach our moderation dashboard, where offending reviews are hidden
  and abusive accounts suspended.

ACCOUNT DELETION is in-app for every role: side menu > Delete Account for
customers; Profile > Delete Account for captains and merchants.

LOCATION. Background location is requested for the captain role only, and only
during an active delivery, so the waiting customer sees the courier move. It
stops when the delivery ends. Customers never request it.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
Google Maps SDK and Places API (maps, search, geocoding, routes); Google
Sign-In (optional login); Firebase Cloud Messaging (push); BRQ SMS gateway
(passcodes); WhatsApp Business API (opt-in notifications); SMTP email
(password reset); MongoDB Atlas (database).
No payment processor: payment is cash to the courier on delivery, and the app
never collects card or bank details. No AI service, no ad network, and no SDK
tracking users across apps or websites - matching our App Privacy answers,
where tracking is declared "No".

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None. A single build, identical features and content everywhere. The service
operates in two Sudanese cities, and a user's city decides which couriers and
stores they see, but no feature, screen or content is enabled or disabled by
region or App Store storefront.

6. REGULATED INDUSTRY / THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
Not a regulated industry: a general courier and local-delivery service. No
financial services, healthcare, gambling, or pharmaceuticals sold by us. Some
listed stores are pharmacies, but the app only delivers for them; it does not
sell, prescribe or describe medicines. All content is our own or uploaded by
merchants through their own accounts, under terms granting us the right to
display their store name, products and images. No protected third-party
material is used.
