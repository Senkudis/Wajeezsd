2. PURPOSE AND TARGET AUDIENCE
Wajeez is a local delivery service in Sudan (Khartoum and Port Sudan). Today
deliveries are arranged by phone: prices haggled verbally, no way to see where
the parcel is, no record if something goes wrong. Wajeez replaces that. A
customer marks pickup and drop-off on a map and says what they need; nearby
couriers ("captains") accept the price or propose another; the customer
chooses, follows the captain live on the map, and pays cash on delivery.
Customers can also browse local stores, order products, and chat.
Audience: adults in Sudan needing parcels, groceries or restaurant orders
delivered, and small merchants selling beyond walk-ins. Rated 4+, no
age-restricted content. A public consumer app, not an employee-only one.

3. SETUP AND ACCESS
One binary; the role comes from the account signed in. Sign in with phone
number and password.
- Customer: [CLIENT_PHONE] / [PASSWORD] - use this for the main review. Set
  pickup and drop-off on the map, choose a price, submit; the order appears
  under "My Orders" with live tracking.
- Captain: [CAPTAIN_PHONE] / [PASSWORD] - available orders, accepting a job,
  delivery. Already approved; new couriers must upload ID, selfie, personal
  and vehicle photos and are approved manually by us.
No sample files needed; every feature works without payment.

CONTENT REPORTING AND BLOCKING (Guideline 1.2). The app shows user-written
store reviews, so both mechanisms exist:
- Report a review: open a store, tap "Customer Reviews", tap the report
  button under a review, pick a reason, submit.
- Report or block a user: open a chat, tap the header menu, then report abuse
  or block user. Blocking stops messages in both directions.
- Reports reach our moderation dashboard, where offending reviews are hidden
  and abusive accounts suspended.

ACCOUNT DELETION is in-app for every role: side menu > Delete Account
(customers); Profile > Delete Account (captains).

LOCATION. Customers: foreground only, to show nearby stores and set the
drop-off pin. Captains: background location during a work shift - from the
moment the captain switches to "Online" until they switch it off - so nearby
orders reach them and the waiting customer can watch the courier move while
the app is backgrounded. None is collected while a captain is offline; the
customer role never uses background location. This matches the permission text
shown in the app.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
Google Maps SDK and Places API (maps, search, geocoding, routes); Google
Sign-In (optional); Firebase Cloud Messaging (push); BRQ SMS (passcodes);
WhatsApp Business API (opt-in); SMTP email; MongoDB Atlas.
No payment processor: payment is cash to the courier on delivery; the app
never collects card or bank details. No AI service, no ad network, and no SDK
tracking users across apps or websites - matching our App Privacy answers,
where tracking is declared "No".

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None. A single build, identical everywhere. The service operates in two
Sudanese cities and a user's city decides which couriers and stores they see,
but no feature or content is enabled or disabled by region or storefront.

6. REGULATED INDUSTRY / THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
Not a regulated industry. A general courier and local-delivery service: no
financial services, no healthcare, no gambling, and no pharmacies or medicines
anywhere in the app - that category was removed entirely and cannot be
selected, searched or ordered. All content is ours or uploaded by merchants
through their own accounts, under terms granting us the right to display their
store name, products and images. No protected third-party material is used.
