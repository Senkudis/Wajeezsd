# Creative production workflow

Applies to every video, edit, ad, animation, infographic, product demo or visual design request. Skills are listed in `.claude/CREATIVE_SKILLS_STATUS.md`.

## Routing

Pick only the skills the request needs; combine when necessary.

- **motion-broll**: talking-head videos, transcript-timed overlays, animated B-roll, cutaways.
- **object-separation**: rotoscoping, background removal, isolated products or speakers, compositing.
- **explainer-high-energy**: short ads, launch videos, hype promos, reels, teasers.
- **explainer-default**: calm premium product demos, app walkthroughs, homepage explainers.
- **d3-viz**: charts, dashboards, data stories, infographics from real numbers.
- **canvas-design**: visual direction, style frames, posters, static infographics, art direction.
- **sound-effects**: every video with animation, transitions, UI actions, kinetic type or a product demo.

"Start editing", "make an ad", "create an animation", "make an infographic", "make a product video", "improve this video": inspect the available assets first, then route.

## Steps

1. **Brief**: audience, platform, format, duration, subject, main message, desired action, brand colours, fonts, logo, references, available assets, things to avoid.
2. **Questions**: if critical information is missing, ask one concise batch. Otherwise assume and state the assumption.
3. **Creative direction**: concept, visual language, colour system, typography, composition, camera, motion, editing rhythm, sound direction, and why it fits the audience and goal.
4. **Script or narrative structure.**
5. **Beat sheet**: scene, start, end, line or on-screen text, visual action, camera, transition, sound effect, music behaviour, purpose.
6. **Asset list**: each asset marked real, supplied, generated or illustrative.
7. **Approval gate**: show direction and beat sheet before any long or expensive render, unless told to proceed without approval.
8. **Produce**, then a **review version**: preview render, contact sheet / key frames, timing review, text and spelling check, brand check, audio check (loudness and peaks measured), mobile readability.
9. **Critique** as creative director, senior editor, motion designer, brand designer, sound designer and audience member. If it reads generic, crowded or amateur, refine by removing and re-spacing, not by adding effects.
10. **Final export.**

## Deliverables

Creative direction, script, beat sheet, asset list, production files, preview render, final export, QC notes, credits/licences, and how to revise.

## Hard rules

- Never invent statistics, prices, customer counts, testimonials, reviews, quotes, logos, certifications, performance results or features. Use real data only when supplied or from an official source the user authorised; label illustrative data.
- One idea per scene; one primary message, one main visual, one supporting detail.
- Intentional motion: controlled easing, anticipation, clear entrances and exits, readable timing, motivated transitions. No random zooms, excess particles, constant glow, glassmorphism, bouncy easing, AI filler visuals.
- Deterministic timing, seeded randomness only; every key frame must render correctly on its own. Check text for clipping, overlap, spelling and safe margins.
- Infographics: find the story first, correct encoding, limited palette, labels and units, honest axes and proportions.
- Sound: voice-over > SFX > music. Hit visual events on the beat or cue. Licensed sounds only (bundled CC0 library), no cheap beeps or identical repeats, duck music under dialogue, measure loudness and true peak, write a cue list and CREDITS file.
- "Make it more professional" means better concept, hierarchy, pacing, typography, spacing, colour discipline, transitions and sound, not more effects.
- "Use a better style" means propose three distinct art directions, explain the differences, recommend one.

## Wassili brand constraints (from project memory)

- Fonts: Alarabiya (Arabic), Arabic Modern Bold (secondary Arabic), Intro (English). Nothing else in brand work.
- Ads always show both App Store and Google Play badges.
- No emojis in UI or ad graphics.
- Speaker ads: no boxes over the speaker; use full cutaway scenes and eye-level framing.
- Tutorial videos: 5 minutes max.
